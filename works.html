Selected Works

Three residential studies, each presented through drawings, plans, and lived context.

01

Waterloo House

Vivid axonometric storytelling for an urban family house.

This project balances a compact footprint with strong visual identity. The drawings foreground garden occupation, street presence, and layered internal planning.

02

Hornsby Heights House

A bush-edge home read through plan geometry and sheltered outlooks.

The project is anchored by site planning and sectional clarity, then expanded by softer perspective views that communicate atmosphere and retreat.

03

Baulkham Hills

A suburban transformation study grounded in the existing house and its next possibilities.

Existing photos, measured elevation, and plan information are combined to frame a project that starts from what is there and then pushes toward a more articulated future.
