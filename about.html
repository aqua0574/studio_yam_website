About Studio Yam

We treat drawings as architecture in progress, not just presentation material.

Studio Yam is a design practice focused on residential architecture, interiors, and spatial transformations that feel distinctive without becoming overstated. We work carefully with scale, circulation, daylight, and material atmosphere so that the final spaces are both memorable and easy to live in.

The studio’s visual language matters. Plans and sections are developed with the same attention as perspective views and public-facing imagery, because clients, councils, consultants, and builders all need different ways into the same idea. That is where clarity becomes part of the design service.

Our projects often begin with an existing house, a difficult site, or an inherited condition that needs to be re-read. We look for the latent order in each place, then use architecture to sharpen it.

Principle One

Context First

Principle Two

Graphic Clarity

Principle Three

Warm Precision
